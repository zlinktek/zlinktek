Choose a series based on your operating environment, interfaces and data load.

## Which Series Fits Your Application?

**Start with the environment, then consider interfaces and data rates:**

1. **High-G loads, flight vibration or landing impacts → JR100:** for aircraft, UAVs and recovery tests.
2. **Multiple serial ports, high-speed Ethernet or Modbus in industrial environments → IR200:** select a configuration for the required interfaces, protocols and recording load.
3. **Conventional serial or low-speed Ethernet recording → IR100:** for basic recording, laboratory debugging and cost-sensitive projects.
