ZLinkTek develops intelligent communication and data-recording equipment for industrial and aerospace applications. Our team brings more than ten years of engineering experience from international technology companies and leading aerospace institutes.

## What We Do

- Industrial multi-interface data recording
- Isolated and unattended data acquisition
- High-G, shock-resistant recording for flight and unmanned platforms
- Product customization, system integration, and engineering support

## Engineering Focus

Our work covers serial and Ethernet communication, isolated interface design, embedded storage, network data retrieval, wide-temperature operation, and rugged mechanical protection. Products can be adapted to project-specific interfaces, installation constraints, power supplies, and data formats.

We support customers from initial interface definition through prototype verification, environmental testing, deployment, and field-data analysis.
