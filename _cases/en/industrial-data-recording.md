## Challenge

Industrial and laboratory systems often contain many sensors, controllers, and communication links. Intermittent faults may only occur after weeks of operation and can be difficult to reproduce without complete historical data.

## Solution

An IR Series recorder captures the original communication stream locally while the equipment continues to operate. Engineers can retrieve the data later, align events by timestamp, and inspect the period before and after a fault.

## Result

- Faster localization of intermittent communication and sensor faults
- Continuous local records without depending on an always-connected PC
- Traceable evidence for verification, maintenance, and customer support
