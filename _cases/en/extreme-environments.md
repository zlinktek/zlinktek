## Challenge

Flight and high-impact tests are expensive and may provide only one opportunity to capture the event. The final seconds of data are often the most valuable for failure analysis, yet they are recorded under the most severe mechanical and electrical conditions.

## Solution

JR100 combines isolated interfaces, rugged mechanical construction, and local non-volatile storage. The recorder configuration is evaluated against the mission shock direction, mounting space, temperature range, power supply, and recovery method.

## Benefits

- Preservation of critical data through severe events
- Support for flight-test reconstruction and root-cause analysis
- Reduced risk from vibration, shock, temperature, and grounding differences
